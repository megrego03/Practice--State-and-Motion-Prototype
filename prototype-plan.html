State and Motion Prototype Plan

Purpose and state map

Specimen User need States Visual/ motion feedback
Action Identify an interactive link and understand when it is hovered, focused, or activated. Default, hover, focus-visible, active Background, border, underline, focus outline, and decorative arrow change to provide clear interaction feedback.
Selection Clearly identify whether the checkbox choice is selected. Unchecked, focus-visible, checked The selected label gains a stronger border and inset mark so the state does not depend on color alone.
Disclosure Understand when additional information has been opened. Closed, open The revealed confirmation panel has a distinct border and surface treatment, with a short finite entrance animation.

Effect decisions

Effect 1 and purpose: A subtle gradient distinguishes the action link from the surrounding surface and reinforces its interactive appearance.

Effect 2 and purpose: An inset box shadow creates a visible structural marker when the checkbox is selected.

What remains with effects removed: The action still has a border, text label, and underline, while the selected choice still has a thicker border. The interface remains understandable without the gradient or shadow.

Motion decisions

Transition properties and trigger: The action link transitions its color and transform over .2 seconds during hover and active interaction states.

Keyframe purpose and trigger: A short reveal animation provides visual feedback when the disclosure is opened. It runs once when the details element enters its open state.

Reduced-motion alternative: When reduced motion is preferred, the transition and reveal animation are removed. The action, selection, and disclosure states remain understandable through borders, text, surface changes, and other static visual feedback.
